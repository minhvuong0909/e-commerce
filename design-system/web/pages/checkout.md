# Page Override: Checkout & Cart Flow

> Overrides `design-system/web/MASTER.md` for the Cart & Checkout Pages (`/user/cart`, `/user/checkout`, `/user/orders/*`).

---

## Conversion & Trust Direction
- **Confidence Building:** Prominent trust badges (SSL 256-bit, 100% genuine guarantee, fast dispatch).
- **Stepping:** Clear 3-step numbered progress bar (1: Address & Map, 2: Carrier & Quotes, 3: Payment Gateway & Review).
- **Sticky Summary:** Floating or sticky order summary with instant breakdown of subtotal, voucher discounts, shipping fees, and final grand total.
- **PayOS Integration:** High visual clarity for VietQR and instant payment confirmation status badge.
